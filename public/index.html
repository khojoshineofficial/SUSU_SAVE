How it works

Create or join a group

Set the amount, the frequency and the number of members, then share your invite code.

Contribute every cycle

Pay daily or weekly from your wallet. Reminders arrive before every due date.

Collect your payout

When your turn comes, the whole pool lands in your wallet — in the order your group agreed.

Withdraw to mobile money

Move your money to MTN, Telecel or AirtelTigo whenever you need it.
