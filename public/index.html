Frequently asked questions

What exactly is a SUSU group?

A group of people who each contribute the same amount on a fixed schedule. Each cycle, the whole pool goes to one member, following an agreed order, until everyone has received a payout.

Who decides the payout order?

The organizer. It can follow the order members joined, or be arranged manually. Once the group starts, the order is locked so nobody can be moved unfairly.

What happens if someone misses a contribution?

The cycle is marked as missed and the member is reminded. Organizers can see outstanding amounts, and a group can be configured to hold a payout until the pool is complete.

How do I get my money out?

Request a withdrawal to your mobile money number. The fee and the amount you will receive are shown before you confirm, and you are notified when it has been sent.

Is my money safe?

Every movement of money is written to an append-only ledger, and balances are calculated on the server from that ledger — never from anything your browser sends.

Can my company or school use this?

Yes. Register an organization account and you get a private space for your members, groups and reports, fully isolated from every other organization on the platform.
